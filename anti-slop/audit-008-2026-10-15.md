## Rule-cited decisions and assessment

- C-1/R-31: existing white/ivory/charcoal/gold semantic colors preserve KAY's
  selected baseline. Typography stays the established system font; no unlicensed
  font or generated image is introduced. Parent-owned gaps and reading/control
  dimensions keep ordinary forms/tables consistent. Flat regions distinguish
  programme, structure, type and physical stock without decorative nested cards.
- C-2/R-24/R-26: hierarchy links lead to actual filtered physical units. Templates
  have no price/availability/reservation controls. Whole campaigns remain a named
  separate target. Future files have truthful text and no dead upload button.
- C-2/C-4/R-27/R-35: empty/loading/error/retry/denied/invalid/conflict states and
  retained drafts are implemented. Pending aggregate controls use the existing
  native fieldset guard. Current versions require an explicit comparison. A real
  pre-fix regression exposed wrong-record RHF defaults; record-keyed editor
  lifetimes now pass block/floor/type/Create switching. That integrity correction
  was independently authorized by the sprint, not an audit-only redesign.
- R-25/R-32: unchanged semantic contrast checked with the installed checker through
  the backend venv. On white: secondary text6.48:1,link/focus6.50,input outline4.54,
  success6.17; corresponding ivory pairs remain above their required thresholds.
  State words accompany color. Native labels, invalid associations/focus, Radix
  arrow-key tabs, keyboard navigation and touch actions retain system behavior.
- C-5/R-17/R-23/R-36: displayed counts derive from PostgreSQL, separately counting
  programmes, actual units and campaign levels. Synthetic records are explicitly
  test fixtures. No invented customer/production/security/performance claim,
  stock-template count or decorative chart is presented as real data.
